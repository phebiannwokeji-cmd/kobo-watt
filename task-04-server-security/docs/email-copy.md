# Kobo Watt — Verification Email Copy

## Authorship statement

I did not write this email copy. The agent (Google Gemini) drafted all of the
wording below. In a real Kobo Watt business I would have a developer or a
marketer review and rewrite it, because email copy is marketing material and
directly affects customer trust. An automated draft should not go to real
customers without human review.

## What I would change before sending to real customers

1. **Tone and voice review.** "The Kobo Watt team" is generic. A real brand
   voice document would specify whether the sign-off should be warmer, more
   formal, or use a specific name or role.

2. **Localisation.** Many Kobo Watt customers are based in Nigeria. The copy
   should be reviewed by a Nigerian English speaker for natural phrasing. If
   Yoruba or Pidgin translations are planned, they should be prepared by a
   fluent speaker, not a machine translation.

3. **Legal and compliance wording.** A privacy or data-handling sentence may be
   required depending on the applicable regulations (e.g., NDPR in Nigeria,
   GDPR if EU customers are served). A solicitor or compliance officer should
   advise on this.

4. **"No action is needed" wording for non-account-holders.** The current
   phrasing is reassuring but does not tell someone who is being impersonated
   where to report the issue. A contact email or support link would be better.

5. **Subject line testing.** "Verify your Kobo Watt email" is functional but
   may land in spam filters. A marketer with deliverability experience should
   A/B test subject lines before launch.

6. **Code formatting in plain text.** The plain-text fallback shows the code
   inline. Some email clients wrap long lines. A short code (six digits) fits
   on one line, but this should be tested in real clients.

7. **Brand name consistency.** "Kobo Watt" appears with a capital W. This
   capitalisation should match whatever is recorded in the brand guidelines.
   The current usage is consistent with Task 1, but a final human check is
   sensible before launch.
