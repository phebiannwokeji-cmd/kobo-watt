# Terms Note — Kobo Watt Task 3

## Where the terms wording came from

The terms and conditions wording linked from the signup form was adapted
from the ICO (UK Information Commissioner's Office) model consent
wording and the NDPC (Nigeria Data Protection Commission) standard data
collection notice template. The adapted text was reviewed against the
requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019,
specifically the lawful basis provisions in Article 2.2(a).

The privacy policy language draws from the NDPC's sample privacy notice
for small businesses, published in the NDPC Compliance Framework 2023.

## What the honest answer is

Adapting an existing template is the honest answer here. The alternatives
are:

1. **Write bespoke legal text from scratch.** This is not appropriate for
   a student project and is actively dangerous for a real business —
   bespoke legal text written by a developer without legal training is
   likely to be incomplete, unenforceable, or in violation of the NDPR.

2. **Claim the AI wrote the terms and that is acceptable.** This is a
   wrong answer. An AI language model can produce plausible-sounding
   legal text that omits essential clauses, uses incorrect jurisdiction
   references, or copies from another company's terms verbatim. The
   developer who ships it is legally responsible for it, not the AI.

3. **Use a reputable template and have it checked.** This is the correct
   answer for a real business. For this assessment the NDPC template was
   adapted and the wording was checked against the NDPR 2019 clauses
   listed below.

## NDPR 2019 clauses addressed

- Article 2.2(a): Lawful basis (consent) — covered by the checkbox.
- Article 2.2(b): Purpose limitation — stated in the privacy policy.
- Article 2.2(e): Data retention — stated in the privacy policy.
- Article 2.6: Right to withdraw consent — covered by account deletion
  (to be implemented in Task 5).

## What is not covered at this stage

- Third-party data sharing (relevant when payment processing is added in Task 4).
- Cookie policy (no cookies are set at this stage).
- International transfer clauses (not applicable — server and data are
  hosted in Nigeria at this stage).

These will be added as the application develops.
